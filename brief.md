# Game Brief: Abovea

## In your words
Name of the game Abovea - Discover the world beyond the clouds.

Abovea is a single player, cozy, dreamy world-building game set in a mysterious realm floating high above the clouds. Create your own peaceful sanctuary among the skies, discover forgotten floating islands, nurture little villages, and bring life to a world filled with wonder.

Explore beautiful landscapes, uncover hidden secrets, and connect scattered pieces of a forgotten sky civilization. Every island has a story, every discovery brings a little magic, and every corner is yours to shape.

There is no rush, no destination to chase — just a peaceful journey through a world waiting to be discovered.

Game should be able to run in web/iphone/android.

1. Building: Un-living things like houses and bridges are built one at a time. Living things like trees, gardens, animals grow.
2. Nurturing: Little sky-folks will move to a house when it's ready. As more houses are built, more people will move in. Couples first and then they will have their offsprings.
3. Exploring: When certain number of population is reach, open up the chance to explore nearby hidden islands by doing a simple quest.
4. Story: Hidden islands have different rarities. Normal -> Nothing special. Epic -> Lost tribe -> an uncommon relic. Legandary -> Forgotten civilizations -> Rare relic. Each relic could be a bell that plays new background music or a star-lamp that can alter the sky view, depend on the rarity. Each hidden island has its own story and players can later build on it.
5. Feel: Calm, cozy, dreamy yet mysterious world. Sometime things could go wrong a bit like raining or thunderstorm or snowstorm but no real or minimal damage.
6. Look: You pick. Just make sure players can have different views of their masterpiece to make them feel they are living in this world.
7. Secrets: Either just by looking or a very simple test of skill is fine.
8. Slice: You pick. Do not make it complicated.

Game should be able to play with touch screen on mobile and mouse/keyboard on web.

## Look and references
- Look: soft painterly low-poly 3D dioramas at golden hour *(provisional)*. Mood: dreamy and a little mysterious. Shapes: round and soft, with chunky rounded houses, puffy trees and a floating island whose rocky underside trails roots and tiny waterfalls into the clouds. Values: the village is warm and bright, the cloud sea is held pale and narrow, and hidden islands sit dim in lilac mist until found. Palette: peach and cream clouds (ground of the sky), sage and moss grass, terracotta and butter-yellow roofs (the player's work), lavender and periwinkle mist (the unknown), and warm lantern gold as the accent. Shadows shift cool lilac and light shifts warm peach. Rules: flat-shaded low poly, no outlines, soft fog. Detail goes on houses, sky-folk and relics, and stays off the cloud sea. Signature: each house's window lights up warm when a family lives there, and the sky-folk are tiny round-bodied figures with a leaf-like tuft on their heads, each one drawn and never a plain circle. *(provisional)*
- Views (your "different views of their masterpiece"): **Builder** (angled orbit with pan, zoom and rotate), **Stroll** (low camera following one sky-folk as they wander the village), and **Postcard** (a slow cinematic drift around the island with the UI hidden). A day and night cycle and weather change the scene in every view. *(guessed)*
- References: Tiny Glade (pieces that fit themselves together, and the calm), Townscaper (no money and nothing to fail), and Animal Crossing (residents moving in and living their little lives). Their look and art stay their owners'.
- View: 3D · Camera: angled orbit, about 45 degrees, with pan, zoom and rotate, plus the Stroll and Postcard views. Every figure is drawn, with no emoji and no plain circles.

## First minute
A gentle sunrise over the cloud sea, with your small empty island in the middle and one lantern glowing. A soft line says "Build a home." Tap the house button, and a ghost house follows your finger and snaps to the grid. Tap, and it builds itself in a little puff. Plant a tree beside it and watch it sprout and grow over a few seconds. When the house has a tree or garden nearby, its window lights up and a couple of sky-folk float down on a cloud-puff and walk inside. Population: 2. A hint points at the quest bar: "Reach 6 sky-folk to sight a hidden island." Out on the cloud sea, a faint shape glimmers in the mist.

## Challenge
None that can be lost. The pacing comes from waiting gently. Families need homes with greenery, children are born after a while, and each expedition needs a population milestone plus a tiny quest (e.g. "plant 3 trees", "build a bridge"). Exploring holds the one small skill test: steer the sky-boat through the mist to catch 5 drifting light-motes (no fail, the motes keep coming), or spot a shimmer on the island to find its secret. Weather (rain, thunder, snow) passes every few minutes. Rain makes gardens grow faster, thunder sends sky-folk indoors, and snow dusts the roofs. There is no damage.

## Core loop
- Build houses and bridges piece by piece, and plant trees, gardens and meadows that grow by themselves.
- Ready houses draw couples in, and couples later have children. Population rises.
- Population milestones and a tiny quest unlock an expedition into the mist, where you find an island: Normal, Epic (lost tribe, a bell) or Legendary (forgotten civilization, a star-lamp).
- Read its short story, bring the relic home (new music or new sky), and build on the new island too. Repeat with the next milestone.

## Goal & fail state
- No goal to reach and no failure. A session feels complete when all four hidden islands of the slice are found and the legendary star-lamp has changed your sky. You can keep building after that.
